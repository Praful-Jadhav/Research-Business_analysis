# Research and Business Analysis — Zero-Trust Evidence Contract

This public repository is a research and decision-support surface, not a substitute for primary evidence.

## Default trust is zero
AI-generated analysis, citations, extracted summaries, metrics and prior audit statements are hypotheses until source provenance and material claims are independently checked. A polished report or a few lines of source references do not prove an analysis is reliable.

## Required claim discipline
- Separate source facts, analyst interpretation, assumptions, recommendation and unverified hypotheses.
- Record source name/location, dates/versions where relevant, evidence used and material limitations.
- Never invent quantitative results, client/customer outcomes, revenue, adoption, credentials or sources.
- State explicitly what a case study does not prove, including implementation, runtime, performance, adoption and commercial outcomes not actually evidenced.
- Do not publish private client data, credentials, confidential contracts or identifying personal information.
- Avoid claiming causation from correlation or recommendations as validated outcomes.

## Material-change evidence
Updates to case studies, README public claims, research scripts or workflows require a packet under docs/evidence/ stating claim ID, source provenance, risk, scope, verification, validation, limitations and exact revision. CI checks evidence structure and required sections, not the factual truth of every claim.

## Status discipline
DRAFT, IN_PROGRESS, BLOCKED, FAILED, VERIFIED, VALIDATED, ACCEPTED, ACCEPTED_RISK and DEFERRED are distinct. Missing evidence is UNKNOWN. A passing lint/test run does not verify citations or validate a business recommendation.

Research is accepted only when material claims can be traced to their sources, important uncertainties are explicit and any implementation/business outcome is independently validated.
